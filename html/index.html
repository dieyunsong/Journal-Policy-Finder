Journal Policy Finder

This is a general discovery tool for researchers to look up a journal's open-access options, embargo & self-archiving (green OA) rules, and article processing charge (APC) information — with links to the publisher's pages.

This tool complements the Journals with Article Processing Charge (APC) Agreements tool , which provides discount details on the ~4,500 journals that have APC agreements with Northwestern. This Journal Policy Finder is for broader searches when you are not limited to APC options. It covers 87 publishers and approximately 52,700 journals (~72% of indexed article volume by OpenAlex), including the titles in the APC discovery tool.

The purple checkmark flags journals with APC agreements with Northwestern to help you filter results more effectively.

To browse by discipline, select up to three subject tags. There is no catalogue of journals by discipline. This tool uses Google Scholar–style taxonomy enriched with OpenAlex journal tagging. This is a work-in-progress, and we are open to your feedback and contribution.
