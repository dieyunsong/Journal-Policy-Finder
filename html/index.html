Journal Policy Finder

This tool helps Northwestern-affiliated authors find each journal's publisher policies in one place: how to publish open access (OA), the publisher's embargo and article sharing (green OA / self-archiving) rules, and where to look up Article Processing Charges (APCs). Every link points to the publisher's (or journal's) own page, so the authoritative source is always one click away — useful when checking compliance with funder and federal public-access mandates.

Considerations:

  • Links, not values. Because publisher terms change and vary by article type, this tool links you to the current policy rather than quoting a number that could go stale. Confirm the specifics (embargo length, APC amount, licence) on the linked page.
  • Some links are publisher-level. A few publishers (e.g., Springer Nature, Wiley, ACM) publish one policy that applies across their journals; others (e.g., Company of Biologists, Cold Spring Harbor, Cogitatio) have per-journal pages. The Notes column flags anything you should double-check.
  • When searching common terms in titles, the results list can be long. Try searching by eISSN for a direct match, or adjust 'Items per page' to see more titles at once.

See Open Access Publishing at Northwestern for library agreements, or email scholarlycommunication@northwestern.edu with questions.

About the data

Each row is one journal from a publisher with which Northwestern (or the Big Ten Academic Alliance) has an open access agreement. The policy links were located from each publisher's official pages and verified during compilation; where a journal-specific page exists it is used, otherwise the publisher-wide page is linked and noted. Where a link could not be grounded with confidence it is left blank rather than guessed. The columns:

  • Journal Title — the journal; where an eISSN links out, it goes to the journal's record on the ISSN portal.
  • eISSN — the electronic ISSN, linking to the ISSN portal (associated ISSNs under “Identifiers”, journal website under “Links”).
  • Publisher — the publisher whose policies apply; use the filter above the table to narrow to specific publishers.
  • Journal Website — the journal's own homepage, where journal-specific details (including per-journal APCs for some publishers) live. Blank when it could not be resolved with confidence.
  • Open Access Options — the publisher's page describing how to publish OA (gold / hybrid / green routes).
  • Embargo & Sharing — the publisher's self-archiving / green-OA / repository-deposit policy, including embargo length. This is the key page for public-access compliance.
  • APC Info — where to find Article Processing Charge amounts (a price list, calculator, or the journal's fees page).
  • Notes — caveats: whether a link is publisher-level, embargo specifics, or why a journal website is blank.

Policies change; always confirm the current terms on the linked publisher page before relying on them.
